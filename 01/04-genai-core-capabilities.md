## 🚀 Core Capabilities of Generative AI

Generative AI transforms EDA through three key capabilities:

1. **Summarization**  
2. **Anomaly Detection**  
3. **Visualization**  

---

## 🧾 1. Summarization

Generative AI can:

- Analyze large datasets instantly  
- Provide clear and concise summaries  
- Highlight key trends and patterns  

➡️ Benefit:
- Eliminates the need for manual data scanning  
- Saves significant time  

---

## 🔍 2. Anomaly Detection

AI acts like a smart detector:

- Identifies unusual data points  
- Flags patterns that don’t match expectations  

### Importance:
- Critical in fields like:
  - Healthcare  
  - Finance  

➡️ Helps prevent risks and detect issues early  

---

## 📊 3. Visualization

Generative AI enables:

- Creation of visuals using simple prompts  
- Transformation of raw data into easy-to-understand insights  

➡️ Outcome:
- Better collaboration  
- Faster decision-making  

---

## ⚡ Why It Matters

These capabilities help:

- Save time  
- Reduce human error  
- Improve analysis accuracy  
- Focus on decision-making instead of manual work
